178-2 The Princess and the Cursed Flower

(Character: Tsukasa Tenma, Emu Otori, Nene Kusanagi, Rui Kamishiro)

[Nonogi Park]

Tsukasa: One, two, three, four!
Emu & Nene: Five, six, seven, eight!
Tsukasa: Excellent! Let's move right from warm-up stretches into vocal exercises... Is what I would say, but...
Tsukasa: Rui still isn't here. And he said he was bringing the script today.
Nene: This morning, he said he'd finished and was doing one last check, but... Ah!
Emu: It's Rui! Good morning, Rui!
Nene: You're pretty late. What have you been...huh?
Rui: So, there...use Rembrandt lighting... No need for a blackout at the end...
Emu: Huh?! He walked right past us!
Tsukasa: Wh-What's going on?! Rui! What's the matter?!
Rui: Ah...
Rui: Haha, forgive me. I was thinking about my plans for the direction while I walked, and it seems I ended up being completely absorbed.
Tsukasa: I see... That certainly does sound like you, but... You should be careful not to get hurt, you know?
Tsukasa: Now then. How did the script turn out?
Rui: I believe I've poured everything I've learned so far into it.
Nene: Someone sounds confident.
Rui: Hehe. I've made full use of everything I experienced and learned through the workshop, after all.
Tsukasa: That's good, then! Let's get to reading!
Rui: Of course!
(Black cut)
Tsukasa: “The Princess and the Cursed Flower”...
Emu: Ooh, so the main character is a princess!
Rui: ArcLand has upheld the motto “anyone can be a princess” since its founding.
Rui: That same concept is also the theme for this contest...
Tsukasa: So how each troupe interprets it will be the deciding factor.
Rui: Yes, exactly.
Tsukasa: Hmm...
Tsukasa: (The early parts of the play are comedic, but... I see. Things begin to change around the middle.)
Tsukasa: (That will call for a change in our acting approaches, I'm sure. And from there...)
Tsukasa: (I see... So this is how the plot develops.)
Tsukasa: (But what happens to the prince after that? Surely...)
Tsukasa: ...
Tsukasa: Rui!!!
Rui: Yes?
Tsukasa: This script is...fascinating!!!
Tsukasa: I was considering my acting choices up to around the middle, but by the end I was wholly engrossed. Well done!
Nene: Yeah! I really liked the message that anyone can be a princess if they guide the one they love.
Emu: Let's start our read-through right away! Oh, but we have to do our vocal warm-ups first!
Rui: Thank you all. Hearing you say that makes all the thought I put into it worth it.
Rui: Now, let's begin as soon as we've warmed up!
(Black cut)
Nene: It looks like everyone's ready. Here we go!
Tsukasa: Yes!
Tsukasa: (The main character of this show is the princess, Nene's role.)
Tsukasa: (I play the deuteragonist, the prince. Ordinarily, one would expect a love story between the two...)
Tsukasa: (But this show begins after that.)
Tsukasa: “Princess! The Dark Lord lies defeated. I have come to rescue you!”
Nene: “Oh, my prince...! My beloved! Take me from this place, and I'll stay by your side, wherever you may go!”
Rui: “And so the prince and princess were married. And they all lived happily ever after.”
Rui: “However, this is where our story begins. What comes after 'happily ever after'?”
Nene: “Here, take this bread. Make sure to share it with your family.”
Nene: “Phew... That's everything we have to give out today.”
Emu: “Princess Harte! Wonderful work as always, your Highness!”
Emu: “A princess of our kingdom, distributing food to the poor and needy! You truly are an example to us all!”
Nene: “Thank you for saying so. But it's nothing to praise me for.”
Nene: “I'm still a newcomer to this kingdom, after all. This is about all I can do for you.”
Nene: “And with the treasury being used up in the border clashes with our neighbor, the number of misfortunate people will only increase...”
Nene: “Still... *Sigh* Today is the tenth day...”
Nene: “It's been ten whole days since I've seen his face!”
Emu: “My, my, your Highness! If you want to see Prince Blesse so badly, you could pay him a visit yourself instead of working so diligently.”
Nene: “I suppose that's true... But somehow, the idea annoys me.”
Nene: “He used to say such beautiful things about loving me forever and whatnot, but he hasn't paid me a single visit lately.”
Emu: “There's nothing for it, my lady. His Highness will inherit the throne from his father someday, and he must prepare for it.”
Nene: “That's true, but... *Sigh* I think I'll go for a walk to clear my head.”
Emu: “Of course! Do take care!”
Tsukasa: “Harte! Where are you, Harte?”
Emu: “Oh? She just left for a walk not a moment ago.”
Tsukasa: “What?! And just when I was able to make some free time...”
Rui: “Your Highness. You still have quite a lot of work to do. Just where do you think you're going?”
Tsukasa: “Ugh, Nidd...! Very well, I'll be right there...”
Tsukasa: (And so, with the prince's duties taking up his time, the two continue to miss one another.)
Tsukasa: (All the while, an aide that supports Blesse's younger brother plots to steal the throne...)
Tsukasa: (The aide places a curse on him with a flower that grows within the kingdom.)
Tsukasa: (And the prince, now cursed, begins to sink into despair. Then...)
Tsukasa: “Right! That's all our work finished for the day!”
Nene: “Huh? But you have that meeting with the messenger from the neighboring kingdom to negotiate for peace...”
Tsukasa: “It's not as though that will actually improve relations, right?”
Tsukasa: “I'd rather spend some quality time with you, Harte!”
Nene: “Oh...”
Nene: “Are you sure? Somehow, this doesn't seem like you...”
Nene: “But it has been such a long time since we've gone anywhere, just the two of us...”
Tsukasa: “You mentioned wanting to go boating on the lake, didn't you? Today is the perfect weather for a little cruise!”
Nene: “Oh, really? Okay... I'll go change into my new dress!”
Tsukasa: (At first, the princess is overjoyed that the prince is making more time for her.)
Tsukasa: (But at the lake, they meet a fairy, who tells them that the prince has been cursed.)
Tsukasa: (So the princess and prince go on a journey to break the curse. But...)
Emu: “Prince! Hurry up and walk already!”
Emu: “You have to uproot the cursed flower yourself, or the curse will never be broken!”
Tsukasa: “I know that, fairy! But it's as though my body refuses to heed me...”
Tsukasa: “Something dark whispers in my mind. It says that pulling up the flower is pointless. That it won't change anything...”
Nene: “Blesse...”
Rui: (The cursed flower's influence grows as they get closer to it.)
Rui: (The closer the prince gets to breaking the curse, the stronger the feelings of emptiness that assault him...)
Rui: (And by the time they're a stone's throw from their destination, the mountain's summit, he's fully immobilized by despair.)
Tsukasa: “No more...”
Tsukasa: “Thank you, Harte... But this is the end. There's nothing left for me.”
Nene: “Blesse! Come on, Blesse! This isn't like you!”
Tsukasa: “None of this matters.”
Tsukasa: “No matter what I do, humanity will still hate. Countries will still wage war.”
Tsukasa: “No matter how much we wrack our brains, no matter what we try to accomplish... Nothing ever changes.”
Tsukasa: “It's all meaningless.”
Nene: “Blesse!”
Nene: “I won't let you do this!”
Nene: “I want to stay with you, Blesse! I want to see what our future together will be like!”
Nene: “So... I won't ever let you give up on me!”
Nene: “If you feel like everything is meaningless, then we'll find a meaning together!”
Nene: “So get back on your feet!”
Tsukasa: (The princess all but carries the prince up the mountain, and somehow they finally reach the flower.)
Tsukasa: (And finally, the cursed flower is uprooted...)
(Black cut)
Rui: Mm. Excellent.
Rui: It's only our first read-through, but you've already grasped your roles rather well. Honestly, I'm impressed.
Emu: Woohoo!!
Nene: Thank goodness... Hehe, I like this role. I feel like I naturally fit into it, somehow.
Tsukasa: Indeed! Harte's stubbornness is very similar to you!
Nene: And just what is that supposed to mean?
Tsukasa: Hah hah hah! Exactly as it sounds! I'm looking forward to a great performance!
Tsukasa: (In truth, this role is the perfect fit for Nene in many ways. Harte has a grouchy side, but she's passionate and driven, and strong enough to bear hardship.)
Tsukasa: (The role that will require careful attention this time...is mine.)
Tsukasa: (A passionate man driven by great love... I'm familiar with roles like that. However...)
Tsukasa: (As Blesse is affected by the curse, he gradually sinks into despair.)
Tsukasa: (That portrayal must ring true, or no matter how hard Nene works, the show as a whole will feel empty.)
Tsukasa: (A prince gradually losing himself to despair... I must grasp this role completely.)
Emu: Huh? Someone's phone is ringing.
Rui: Oh, that would be mine. Hm?
Tsukasa: What is it?
Rui: It's a call from an unexpected person.
Rui: Asahi.
Tsukasa, Emu & Nene: Huh?!
(Black cut)
