187-7 My Approach

(Character: Kohane Azusawa, An Shiraishi, Akito Shinonome, Toya Aoyagi, Hatsune Miku, Kagamine Len, MEIKO)

[A few days later]

[Crase Cafe]

Len (VBS): Hey, it's Akito!
Akito: Hey there. Is Toya here?
Miku (VBS): He's in the back. An and Kohane are with him.
Akito: Huh? I only called Toya, though.
Toya: I contacted them. These two have also been wanting to see you, Akito.
An: Seriously, if you're doing okay, then answer our texts! Do you have any idea how worried Kohane's been?
Kohane: Oh, um... I'm sure you've been busy.
Kohane: But you haven't even been replying with stamps or anything, so we've been a little worried.
Akito: Ah... Sorry. There's been something I've been focusing on.
MEIKO (VBS): Something you've been focusing on?
Akito: Yes.
Akito: So, Toya, here.
Toya: What's this CD?
Akito: The song I wrote. I basically just threw it together, so it's really rough.
Akito: I wanna ask you to finish it for me.
Toya: I don't mind, but...
An: You just suddenly show up with a new song? Did something happen over there?
Akito: Some stuff, yeah. Actually, I should explain everything that's happened this time around.
Akito: Since it's also got to do with that song.
(Black cut)
Toya: I see. A song that lets you use both English and Japanese to their fullest extents.
Akito: Yeah, since I wasn't able to get my feelings across perfectly in English. Brute-forcing Japanese songs also didn't work.
Akito: So, I changed how I was thinking.
Akito: I'm better at pronouncing Japanese, and it's easier for me to create a rhythm and sing with feeling.
Akito: So, I tried mixing in bits of English at precise points to create a song with a sound and rhythm that can get people going.
Kohane: Bits of English at precise points...?
An: Isn't that basically what we already do with our songs?
Akito: Yeah. But we don't use a ton of English, right?
Akito: And when we write our lyrics, we think them out in Japanese first.
Akito: But this one's gonna be different. I want to write it with both Japanese and English from the start.
Toya: I think I understand now.
Toya: By using both languages and switching between them, you'll be able to emphasize the best of what their sounds and rhythms have to offer.
Kohane: Oh, I see...! If it includes lots of words that people know, then it'll be easier for them to understand the meaning of the lyrics as they listen to them.
An: It'll be hard to switch back and forth like that, but it'll be super cool if you manage to pull it off!
Miku (VBS): That's interesting. I'd like to see what kind of song it'll become.
Akito: Yeah... I can't say for sure if this is the right choice.
Akito: But it's all I've got right now. My skills alone couldn't get me where I needed to go, so...
Len (VBS): Akito...
Akito: Even so, I've gotta do it.
Akito: If we're gonna fight on the world stage, then we'll run into walls like this one again.
Toya: You're right. After all, only one group will win RUSH BEATS and earn that ticket.
Toya: We need to approach future challenges in ways that only we can pull off.
Toya: And this song may be our first step toward achieving that.
Akito: Toya...
Toya: So, leave it to me.
Toya: I'll take all the feelings you've poured into this song and make it a great one.
An: I'm not very confident in my English pronunciation... But if you wanna practice, I'll be right there with you!
Kohane: Yes, I'll sing with you too!
Kohane: You can do this, Shinonome.
Akito: Yeah. Thanks.
Len (VBS): Akito's one thing, but they've all gotten so strong.
MEIKO (VBS): Hehe. You're right. They've gained the strength to believe in someone when they tackle an obstacle... And the strength to ask for help when they need it.
MEIKO (VBS): I'm sure it's because of how strongly they trust each other.
Miku (VBS): For sure. They're bound to run into plenty more obstacles along the way...
Miku (VBS): But they'll probably just keep overcoming or breaking through all of them like this.
Len (VBS): Yeah...
Len (VBS): They're so cool.
(Black cut)
