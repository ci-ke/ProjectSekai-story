Tsukasa: “Huff, huff... We managed to escape the castle, but...”

Tsukasa: But this is an important break in the tension for the audience, as the characters make good their escape! I want to include some exaggerated motion to strike a better contrast!

Nene: What if we included a short dance here?
Tsukasa: What?! You want to add a new dance number at this stage?!
Nene: Yeah. After this, the scene changes to everyone being shocked by the mountain's height, right?
Nene: I think that juxtaposition could be funny...
Emu: Oh... I see! At the start, they're all “let's go, yay yay♪” and then at the end, they're all “nooooo!”
Nene: Yes! Exactly like that!
Rui: I can see it. Considering how the plot develops from there, inserting a humorous beat could provide some levity to help keep the audience's attention.
Rui: I'd like to try it out. What do you all say?
Tsukasa: I swear, we always do this right before showtime. It makes my blood run cold!
Emu: But it'll make things more interesting! Don't you think so too?
Tsukasa: I do! Now then, let's get right to choreography!
(Black cut)

Asahi: (They're all so fired up.)
