184-2 A Failed Work

(Character: Ena Shinonome)

[Ena's Room]

Ena: What do I do...?
Ena: A failed work...
(White cut)

(Memory cut-in)

(Background change)
Ena: A failed work...? What do you mean?
Yukihira: I mean just that. For what I assigned today, I want you to submit a failed work.
Yukihira: However, it must explore the “signpost” theme somehow. As long as it fulfills that requirement, you're free to do as you like.
Ena: U-Umm...
Yukihira: Anyway, I'll be looking forward to seeing your submission.
(White cut)

(Memory cut-out)

(Background change)
Ena: (Mr. Yukihira said he meant just that... But why does he want me to submit a failed work?)
Ena: (I mean, if I'm gonna create something, then I want it to be something that I think looks good.)
Ena: (But I'll just have to give it a try since Mr. Yukihira asked. There has to be some deeper reason...)
Ena: Let's see... A failed work... What comes to mind?
Ena: (Ordinarily, that'd be a piece you toss out because it was done too sloppily... Or a piece that you thought turned out well but didn't get much praise in the end.)
Ena: (So, to me, what is a “failed work”...? I've drawn plenty of things that turned out really sloppy, but there's no way I can submit something like that.)
Ena: (He's always telling me how the drawings' depictions are simple or unimaginative...)
Ena: (To be fair, I still have a habit of getting my perspectives all wrong, and everyone else is way more imaginative than me.)
Ena: (So, am I supposed to emphasize those issues for this piece?)
Ena: (Hmm... But it's hard to deliberately draw something that lacks creativity. Honestly, I don't think I can...)
Ena: Alright, I'll go for a simpler depiction than usual for this.
Ena: My subject will be...
(Black cut)
(Background change)

[The next day]

[Art Class]

Yukihira: I see. You decided to draw the sign for this class.
Ena: Yes. I see it as the signpost leading me to a place where I can draw and learn more about art.
Yukihira: So, you consider this work a failure?
Ena: Yes. Normally, I'd add a lot more shading to make sure my subject stands out from the background. I deliberately didn't do that for this.
Ena: Umm... My goal was to go with an overly simple depiction.
Yukihira: I see... Now I understand.
Yukihira: So, what is the correct way to draw this?
Ena: Huh? Well...
Ena: I'd have to add more contrast to make my subject, the sign, stand out more from the background.
Yukihira: Is that all?
Ena: Huh...?
Yukihira: Is that really the “signpost” you were trying to draw for this piece, Shinonome?
Ena: (If it's a question of whether adding some contrast would make this a good piece...)
Ena: (I don't think it would.)
Ena: (That's just how you're “supposed to” draw something like this.)
Ena: (I haven't really depicted what this “signpost” means to me.)
Yukihira: As you also seem to understand now, Shinonome, you failed to actually touch upon the theme in this piece.
Yukihira: At the very least, it seems to me that you simply chose a subject which is easy to make appear to be a failure.
Ena: ...
Yukihira: You've been asked to create a failed work, but you still have to put in an honest effort.
Yukihira: Please choose a subject which explores the assigned theme and turn that into a serious submission which you can call your own, Shinonome.
Ena: Understood...
(Black cut)
