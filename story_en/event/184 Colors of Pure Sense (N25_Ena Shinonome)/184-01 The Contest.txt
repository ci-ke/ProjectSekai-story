Ena has begun attending her technical mastery course to prepare for art school entrance exams. Though surrounded by overwhelmingly talented artists, her determination to devote herself to her studies is undimmed. A fresh struggle begins when her teacher announces a contest for course attendees.

184-1 The Contest

(Character: Ena Shinonome)

[Art Class]

Yukihira: Alright, that concludes today's practical course. Thank you all for your hard work.
Ena: Phew...
Ena: (I'm exhausted...)
Ena: (As glad as I am about him holding this special course geared towards entrance exams, you have to perform at such a high level...)
Ena: (Both his lectures and our one-on-one meetings are teaching me a lot. But I always have to use my brain a ton...)
Students: Hey, do you already know where you're gonna apply?
Students: Yeah, I'm applying to Tokyo Art University. On top of it being a really prestigious school, I feel like I'll learn a lot.
Students: Me too! It's probably gonna be really hard to get in since there's always so much competition, but I feel like you gotta apply there if you wanna study art.
Ena: (Mr. Yukihira praised her during class yesterday. I didn't know that she's applying to Tokyo Art University.)
Ena: (But she can create amazing works of art, so she might just get accepted.)
Ena: ...
Ena: Tokyo Art University, huh...?
Futaba: A lot of people seem to want to go there.
Ena: Oh... Yeah. You're also applying there, right, Futaba?
Futaba: *Nod* In the end, Tokyo Art University is where I want to attend the most.
Futaba: But I'll have to work really hard since the competition is always so fierce each year.
Ena: Right...
Ena: (They've got amazing facilities and a lot of really famous professors. I also feel I should study there if I'm serious about becoming an artist.)
Ena: (But...)
Futaba: Have you already made a decision, Ena?
Ena: I...
Ena: I'm still thinking it over... I'm not sure yet.
Futaba: Okay...
Futaba: Let me know if you ever need any help. I've been attending open houses, so I have an idea of what things are like at some universities.
Ena: Oh...
Ena: Thanks, I really appreciate it.
Futaba: Of course! I'm also looking forward to working towards our entrance exams together.
Ena: (Tokyo Art University, huh...?)
Ena: (Futaba and those other girls are all really good, so they might actually get accepted there...)
Ena: ...
Ena: (But it's the most selective art school in all of Japan.)
Ena: (Talented students from all over the country attend there. Some got in after taking five or six gap years.)
Ena: (At my current level, I know that I don't have a chance...)
Ena: (But...)
Ena: (I've gotta make the most of Mr. Yukihira's lessons and improve as much as I can right now.)
Ena: (Until I reach a level where I can at least write down Tokyo Art University as one of my prospective schools...!)
(Black cut)

[The next day]

Yukihira: Good morning. Everyone seems to be here, so let's begin.
Yukihira: Starting today, we'll be holding a series of contests in preparation for your practical exams.
Ena: Contests...
Futaba: That means we're going to be ranked based on what we draw. Every year, the students getting ready for exams always seem to have a tough time.
Yukihira: Each piece will receive a grade ranging from an “A+” to a “C-”. Please keep in mind that they will also be arranged around this classroom in order of their rank.
Ena: ...
Ena: (Being ranked means being compared to my peers...)
Ena: (On the other hand...)
Ena: (If I don't do well here, then I won't have any hope of getting into an art school.)
Ena: (Which would make Tokyo Art University an even more impossible goal.)
Ena: (I have to fight like crazy. I have to give it everything I have...!)
Yukihira: Now, let's get into today's assignment.
Yukihira: Today's theme will be “memory”. And your subject will be...
Yukihira: This. Please draw it.
Ena: An apple...?
Yukihira: You are free to draw whatever you like as long as your piece involves these two things.
Yukihira: Now, please begin.
Ena: ...!
Ena: (An apple and memory...?)
Ena: (I have to draw something based on these two things, but...)
Ena: (Memory...?)
Ena: (A memory involving an apple?)
Ena: (Come to think of it, I went apple picking when I was little.)
Ena: (Also, Honami told me about those really amazing apple pie shops...)
Ena: (But neither of those ideas seems very strong.)
Ena: (Some of the others here will be way more creative, so I have to draw something that's going to leave a big impression.)
Ena: (This is so hard...)
Ena: (Just an apple would be easy since I draw those all the time...)
Ena: (Speaking of which, I've been drawing apples since really early on. I've sketched them so many times...)
Ena: (I sketch at least once a day, and I even drew some yesterday.)
Ena: (I might just stand a chance with this idea.)
(Black cut)

[The next day]

Yukihira: Good morning, everyone. I've posted the results of our competition, so please take a look at them.
Ena: (I'm so nervous...)
Ena: (Our pieces are lined up in order of our grade, which is supposed to be on a sticky note, right...?)
Ena: (I gave it absolutely everything I had... I think.)
Ena: (I'm sure I got at least a “C”... Maybe even a “B-”...)
Ena: (“C-”...)
Ena: (That's the lowest grade. Not only that... I'm at the very bottom...)
Students: Wow... That drawing's so amazing...
Students: For a second, I thought it was a photo...
Ena: Huh...?
Ena: ...!
Ena: (Just an apple core...? The background is this classroom, but there's just this depiction of an eaten apple...)
Ena: (The core still looks juicy. And based on the bite marks, you can tell that this person ate as much of it as they could...)
Ena: (I see... It's the artist's memory of when they ate this apple.)
Ena: (Even with the same theme, I don't think I could achieve this level of detail...)
Ena: What made them want to draw this...?
Futaba: It really is incredible... There's no way I can imitate this person...
Ena: Futaba...
Ena: (Wait, what about Futaba's drawing?)
Ena: Is this you eating a paper apple...?
Futaba: Hehe. I was going to draw me eating a real one. But I didn't actually eat one, so I had to go with a fake memory.
Ena: Oh...
Ena: (Right... That's also allowed.)
Ena: You really are amazing, Futaba...
Futaba: No, I'm really not! This apple core drawing was also based on an imaginary scenario, but I didn't go into nearly as much detail...
Futaba: Is this a picture of you sketching an apple? I really like how hard you seem to be working on it.
Ena: Thanks...
Ena: (My drawing deserves to be in last place.)
Ena: (I mean, there's that person's drawing, Futaba's...)
Ena: (And everyone else's. They're all so amazing.)
Ena: (Their imagination and drawing skills are on a completely different level...)
Ena: (Which proves that I don't have what it takes to get into Tokyo Art University.)
Ena: (So, I have to get good enough...!)
Ena: (I have to claw my way up to their level and figure out how to make better grades.)
Yukihira: ...
Yukihira: We will be holding these contests regularly from now on, so please use them to get a better understanding of yourself. I want to see your best efforts.
Yukihira: Now, I'll start handing out your regular assignments. Please have them ready by next time.
Ena: (The theme is a “signpost”...?)
Ena: (I have no idea what I'm going to draw...)
Ena: (But I have to find a way to improve before the next contest. I'll start by coming up with the perfect idea for this.)
Yukihira: Shinonome?
Yukihira: Would you mind coming over here? I'd like to take a minute to speak with you.
Ena: Huh? Y-Yes, of course...!
(Black cut)
Ena: Umm... What is it that you'd like to discuss?
Yukihira: I have a few questions regarding the piece you entered in for this contest.
Ena: Oh, okay...
Yukihira: What made you want to draw this, Shinonome?
Ena: Huh...?
Ena: I... I tried drawing a self-portrait of me sketching some apples.
Yukihira: Were there any other thoughts involved?
Ena: I thought a lot about it... And I became convinced that this idea was my best shot at doing well in the contest.
Yukihira: Your best shot?
Ena: The others outdo me in a lot of respects... I thought I'd do a good job with this since I've sketched so many apples for practice until now...
Yukihira: I see...
Yukihira: It seems that you require a different sort of challenge, Shinonome.
Ena: Huh...?
Yukihira: For the “signpost” assignment I just gave you all...
Yukihira: I'd like for you to submit a failed work.
(Black cut)
