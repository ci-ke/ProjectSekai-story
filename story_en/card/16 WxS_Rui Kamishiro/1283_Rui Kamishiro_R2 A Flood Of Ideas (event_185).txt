1283_Rui Kamishiro_R2 A Flood Of Ideas (event_185)

Skill name: Thanks Met With Thanks

<Side Story (Part 1)>

(Character: Rui Kamishiro, Hatsune Miku, MEIKO)

[Shopping Mall]

Rui: UV glow tape, masking tape... That's about all for that.
Rui: Let's see, what else...
Miku (WxS): <Rui, Rui♪>
MEIKO (WxS): <What are you up to today?>
Rui: Oh, hello, you two. Are you here to visit?
Rui: I'm preparing for our next production. Buying things we might need, you see.
Rui: That said, I'm really just replenishing our stocks at the moment.
Miku (WxS): <Oh, I see! I thought you were looking for parts for your next amazing invention!>
Rui: Hehe, I'm sorry to disappoint.
Rui: I do plenty of shopping for things other than components. Many days I simply pick up odds and ends we need for shows.
MEIKO (WxS): <Still, there was that one time, right?>
MEIKO (WxS): <You went out for supplies and came back with a huge box full of things for new inventions.>
Miku (WxS): <Oh, I remember that☆>
Miku (WxS): <Nene really gave you an earful! She said to call before you buy that much stuff!>
Rui: Ah, yes. I was browsing, when the idea for a new effect came to me in a flash.
Rui: I ended up buying the materials for it then and there. Perhaps that's its own form of impulse shopping.
Miku (WxS): <Ooh, so! Maybe you'll get a bright shiny flash today too♪>
Rui: The possibility certainly isn't zero... But I don't think it'll happen this time.
Rui: Our current production is the same script as for the Show Contest, after all.
Rui: We more or less settled what we need for this show at that time.
Miku (WxS): <Oh, so no parts today then.>
Rui: Yes, all I need is...
MEIKO (WxS): <What's wrong?>
Rui: During the dance scene, I was planning to simply change the background behind them...
Rui: But since it's representing a perilous trek, it might help to include wind and lighting effects...
Miku (WxS): <Um, Rui?>
Rui: Ah, yes, I did see something that seemed useful earlier...
(Black cut)
Rui: Phew. That ought to do it.
Miku (WxS): <Wow! Your cart is filled all the way up to the top!>
MEIKO (WxS): <You just suddenly switched into materials mode. That was a bit surprising.>
Rui: I apologize for startling you.
Rui: While we were talking, a few ideas suddenly came to me.
Rui: This should help turn the dance scene we discussed adding during practice today into something very memorable.
Miku (WxS): <But will you be able to bring all this stuff home all by yourself?>
Rui: It certainly is more than I intended to buy, but I can carry this by myself. I'll be—
Miku (WxS): <Rui?>
Rui: Actually, maybe I could tweak that idea. Perhaps if I... Yes...
Rui: I could also do something for the curtain call. Considering the dimensions of the stage, I'll need...
Miku (WxS): <Rui's eyes are sparkling!>
MEIKO (WxS): <I wonder what he'll buy this time.>
(Black cut)
Rui: There, that should be everything. I'm sorry, you two. I hope you haven't been bored?
Miku (WxS): <Not at all☆>
MEIKO (WxS): <You just kept having new ideas. Shopping with you is very exciting!>
Rui: Hehe, I'm glad to hear that.
Rui: But... It seems I've once again ended up with quite a lot to carry.
MEIKO (WxS): <Right... If this was SEKAI, I could help you carry things...>
Miku (WxS): <We were all worried about it, but MEIKO and I forgot too!>
Rui: Still...
Rui: There's nothing that makes me happier than new ideas for effects.
Rui: And to make those ideas into reality as fast as possible, I'll buckle down and get everything home.
MEIKO (WxS): <You've got this, Rui!>
Miku (WxS): <You can do it! You can do it!>
(Black cut)


<Side Story (Part 2)>

(Character: Nene Kusanagi, Rui Kamishiro)

[Wonderland SEKAI]

Rui: Everything looks fine for now.
Rui: Let's move on to the next check. Can you dance for me, Robo-Nene?
Robo-Nene: Right away!
Nene: She seems really energetic somehow.
Robo-Nene: Thanks to that maintenance, I can dance very precisely!
Rui: I'm glad to hear it.
Nene: I'm sorry, Rui. We only just wrapped our production, and here I am asking you to do a full maintenance pass.
Rui: Not at all. I was thinking about it myself, so your timing was perfect.
Rui: Lately, we've had our training at ArcLand, the Show Contest, and then our production at the Center Theatre. There's been hardly any time for maintenance.
Nene: Yeah. We haven't had a lot of breathing room.
Nene: Especially me... I've been so caught up in my own head.
Nene: ...
Nene: Um... Thanks, Rui.
Rui: Hm? Whatever for?
Nene: Um, you know...
Rui: The maintenance? Of course, I'm happy to do that anytime.
Nene: No, not that...
Rui: No? In that case...
Rui: It must be for the new function I secretly added to Robo-Nene during her last maintenance?
Nene: Huh?
Rui: The aroma diffuser that automatically activates in the evening.
Rui: She's equipped with relaxing scents to promote restful sleep for you.
Nene: Wait, that was you?!
Nene: I noticed there was a nice scent in my room at night, and I thought it was a little weird...
Nene: And then one time I woke up in the middle of the night, and Robo-Nene was just standing by my bed!
Rui: Oh, maybe she wanted to bring the relaxing scents closer to you.
Nene: Whatever, it was scary!
Rui: Hmm. I had intended it to be a pleasant surprise, but I get the feeling you don't care for it much.
Rui: But if you aren't thanking me for that, I can't think of anything else. Could you tell me what you do mean?
Nene: Ugh, listen...! I mean, for telling me to shoulder the responsibility of being an actress!
Nene: I missed my chance to thank you during the production, but I wanted to say it properly...
Rui: Ah... That's what you meant.
Rui: I still don't know if that was the best way to handle the situation.
Rui: It was entirely possible that putting more pressure on you could have worsened your mental state.
Nene: But you still said it.
Nene: Because you knew I could handle it.
Rui: Yes... I did.
Nene: Thank you for believing in me.
Rui: In that case, I have to express my gratitude, too.
Rui: Thank you for rewarding my faith, Nene.
Nene: Yeah.
Nene: I'll keep on doing it, too. As long as you all believe in me.
Robo-Nene: Look at me, you two! I am performing the requested test!
Rui: Ah, my apologies. Yes, I don't see any problems with your dancing.
Robo-Nene: What function should we test next?
Rui: Hmm, next... Why don't you sing?
Nene: Do you think she'll be as impressive at singing as she is at dancing?
Rui: Hehe. We'll have to see.
Robo-Nene: Leave it to me! I won't let you down!
(Black cut)
