Akito: But we just got back from our last trip. I basically know what I need already.
Kohane: But if you forget something this time, we won't be there to lend it to you...

Rin (VBS): Oh, oh! Let us help, too!
Len (VBS): Is there anything we can help you with?
Akito: I mean, not really... I'm more or less ready as it is.
An: In that case, can you two take this list and help Akito go over his suitcase with it?
Rin (VBS): You bet!
Len (VBS): We'll make sure there isn't a single thing missing!
Akito: You guys are really overreacting for a little trip.
MEIKO (VBS): Hehe. They're sending a precious teammate off on his own, after all. They want to make sure they can help.
MEIKO (VBS): Wouldn't you feel the same way if you were in their shoes?
